CRM Testing Lab

Eight rounds, one variable per round. Each test has a hypothesis, a behavioural principle, a sample check before it runs and a decision rule after. What wins goes into the learning log and into the next send.

The method

Rules for every test

  1. Flight 1 is for learning. Each round answers one question about what partners need and value.
  2. Frequency cap. At most one adoption email per partner per week, across all campaigns. Service messages are exempt, and onboarding journeys follow their own timing. A partner can join several rounds over time but never sees the same test twice, and nothing is carried over from one round to the next.
  3. Exclusions. Each send leaves out partners who already subscribed, the holdout, and partners in another test that week. Service messages run without a holdout, since every partner must receive them. A sunset rule leaves out partners who have not opened any of our emails in 90 days: they are reached in-app or by an Account Manager.
  4. One variable per round. Everything else stays the same. Before each send, a second CRM executive checks both cells (buddy check).
  5. Split at random at company level, so every user in a salon gets the same cell, with a test property on the company in HubSpot. Compare cells only within the same group and market.
  6. Write the hypothesis and the principle before the send, and name the group it is tested on.
  7. Check the sample first. If a group is too small for one send, spread Flight 1 over more sends, at least a week apart. Pool markets only when the copy is the same concept in each language, and split cells within each market.
  8. The goal decides: the version that gets more partners to take the action we want wins. Clicks and opens are supporting signals only, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens.
  9. Log a learning when the sample is reached and the result is significant at 95%, or earlier only if the result is overwhelming (p < 0.001). Otherwise partners did not respond differently, and the choice matters less.
  10. Watch the guardrails: unsubscribes and spam complaints under 0.1%, never above 0.3% (Gmail's limit).
  11. Legal and compliance wording is never tested. Only framing, order and timing.
