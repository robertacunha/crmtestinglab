How the score works

Ratings. Each heuristic is rated Missing (0), Partial (1) or Present (2). The copy type decides which heuristics apply: headlines are scored on loss framing, processing fluency and concrete specificity, and other copy types on all six. The readiness score is the average out of 100, with every heuristic weighted the same. Your rating counts first, then the automated suggestion. The score stays a draft until every applicable heuristic is rated. The score is a checklist index. It has not yet been checked against campaign results, so it does not predict CTR or conversion.

What is research and what is tool logic. Processing fluency uses the published Flesch Reading Ease bands: 60 and above is plain English, 30 to 59 is fairly difficult to difficult, below 30 is very difficult. The pattern rules for social proof, CTA friction and concrete specificity, which heuristics apply to headlines, and the verdict bands at 50 and 75 on the ruler, are tool logic, not research findings.
